From zero to your Vow.id

Never had anything to do with crypto before? This guide takes you through it step by step: set up a wallet, secure it, get a little BNB, register with Vow.id and make the tokens visible. Tick off what is done and carry on at your own pace.

Part 1

Set up and secure your wallet

A wallet is an app you use to identify yourself to services like Vow.id and to manage crypto assets. It belongs to you alone. There is no "forgot password": the only spare key is your seed phrase.

  1. 1. Choose a wallet app

    You need a wallet that can handle the BNB Smart Chain. Trust Wallet serves as the example here because it is offered directly in Vow.id's wallet selection. MetaMask or TokenPocket work just the same.

    Choose carefully: your Vow.id stays tied to this wallet forever.

  2. 2. Install the app from the right source

    Go to the provider's official website and follow the link from there to the App Store or Play Store. Do not search for the app freely in the store and do not click on adverts. There are fake wallet apps that steal funds.

  3. 3. Create a new wallet

    Open the app and choose "Create new wallet". Set a lock code for the app. The app now shows you your seed phrase: 12 English words in a fixed order.

  4. 4. Write down the seed phrase

    Write the 12 words by hand on paper, each with its number and in the right order. Check every word. The app then asks you for them once.

    The right wayOn paper, two copies, in two separate safe places. For example in a safe at home and with a person you trust, in a sealed envelope.
    Never thisScreenshot, photo, notes app, email to yourself, cloud, WhatsApp, password manager on the same phone. And never dictate or show it to anyone.

    Whoever has the words has the wallet. Whoever loses them, and the phone as well, has nothing left.

Part 2

Get a little BNB into the wallet

Vow.id runs on the BNB Smart Chain. Every transaction there costs a small fee in BNB. For registration, at least 0.002 BNB must be in your wallet, a very small amount. Take a little more so that it also covers later steps.

  1. 5. Find your address

    In the wallet, tap "Receive" and choose BNB Smart Chain. The app shows a long address that starts with 0x. That is your public address. You may pass it on; it is like an account number. Always copy it with the copy button and never type it out.

  2. 6. Get BNB

    There are two common ways:

    • Buy in the wallet. Many wallets have a "Buy" button. Behind it are payment services that accept card or bank transfer and usually require a short identity check. Choose BNB on the BNB Smart Chain. Compare the fee shown; it is often high for small amounts.
    • Have it sent to you. Someone who already has BNB sends a small amount to your address. Or you buy on a crypto exchange and withdraw from there.

    Watch the network: When sending or withdrawing, "BNB Smart Chain" must be selected, often also called "BSC" or "BEP20". A wrong network is the most common beginner's mistake. The money then does not arrive and is usually lost.

  3. 7. Check that it has arrived

    After a few minutes the amount should appear in your wallet under BNB (BNB Smart Chain). Only continue once you see it there. With larger sums, always send a small test amount first.

Part 3

Register with Vow.id

Now comes the actual step. Open Vow.id for the first time via the invitation link of the person who invited you. Your referrer cannot be changed later.

  1. 8. Open the invitation link and connect your wallet

    Open the link in your phone's browser and tap "Create Vow.id". Choose your wallet or "WalletConnect". The phone switches to the wallet app and asks whether you allow the connection. Confirm and go back to the same browser tab.

  2. 9. Sign the message

    Check the line "Referred by …" and tap "Sign message". Your wallet shows a text and asks for your signature. This signature costs nothing and moves no funds. It only proves that the wallet belongs to you.

  3. 10. Join the affiliate tree

    Tap "Join and open wallet". Now comes your first real transaction: the wallet shows the network fee in BNB. Confirm it once and wait. Do not tap several times, even if it takes a moment.

  4. 11. Confirm your email and secure your name

    Enter your normal email address and type in the four-digit PIN from your inbox. Then choose your handle, that is, your name at Vow.id. It becomes name@vow.id and stays forever.

What comes next is in the Vow.id quick-start guide: first purchase, frequent questions and help when something gets stuck.

Part 4

Make the tokens visible in your wallet

On its own, your wallet only shows well-known tokens. You have to add VOW and the voucher tokens (v$, v€, v£ and others) once so that they become visible. Adding only means "showing". You do not buy anything by doing so and you do not receive anything.

  1. 12. Add tokens via Vow.id

Part 5

Later: exchanging euros for crypto

For registering and collecting rewards you need nothing apart from that little bit of BNB. Buying crypto only becomes a topic when you want to pay for Premium with USDC or USDT or redeem approved rewards with VOW.

  1. 14. Understand the principle before you buy

    • Buying: Using your wallet's buy function or a purchase service, you pay euros and receive, for example, USDT or USDC at your address. Expect an identity check and fees.
    • Swapping: You get VOW by swapping USDT, USDC or BNB on the BNB Smart Chain for VOW via an exchange such as PancakeSwap. Every swap costs a fee, and the price fluctuates.
    • Sort out the way back: Before buying, find out how you get back into euros and what that costs. It is not equally easy with every service and every token.
    • Start small: Make your first purchase with an amount whose loss would not bother you. Crypto assets can fall sharply.

    Which services members use for this is in the quick-start guide under "Where do I get VOW for redeeming?".

Understanding approvals

Sooner or later an app will ask you for an "approval". That is something different from the free signature at sign-in. Vow.id explains the terms in its security section like this:

  • Token approval: You allow another address or a smart contract to spend a certain amount of a token from your wallet. Swap apps need this before they can trade for you.
  • Unlimited approval: The app may move far more than is needed for the one operation, often your entire balance of that token, now and in the future. Many apps ask for this to spare you a second transaction. If your wallet offers it, rather enter only the amount you need right now.
  • Revoke: You can remove an approval later. That costs a small network fee. The app simply asks again the next time you use it.

The wallet scan in Vow.id ("Security" section) shows you your existing approvals. It does not find everything: some signatures exist only outside the blockchain and do not appear in any scan. Revoking also does not bring back anything that is already gone. It is one building block, not all-round protection.

The most expensive beginner's mistakes

  • Entered or photographed the seed phrase. Every page that asks for it is a scam, even if it looks genuine.
  • Chose the wrong network. For Vow.id always BNB Smart Chain (BSC, BEP20).
  • Typed the address instead of copying it. One wrong character and the money is gone. After pasting, compare the first and last four characters.
  • Confirmed a transaction without reading it. A signature for signing in is harmless. A transaction or an approval can move funds. Only confirm what you understand.
  • Responded to "support" in chats. Real support does not message you first and never asks for codes or words.
  • Tapped several times because it hangs. That is how duplicate payments happen. Check first, then try again.

Terms in one sentence

Wallet
The app you use to identify yourself and manage crypto assets.
Seed phrase
12 words that restore your wallet on any device. The master key.
Address
Your wallet's public number, starts with 0x. Anyone may know it.
Network
The blockchain something sits on. For Vow.id, the BNB Smart Chain.
BNB
The currency in which fees are paid on the BNB Smart Chain.
Gas
Another word for the network fee of a transaction.
Token
An asset on a blockchain, for example VOW, v$ or USDT.
Signature
A free signature that proves the wallet belongs to you.
Approval
The permission for an app to move tokens from your wallet.
Transaction
An operation on the blockchain. Costs a fee and cannot be undone.
Stablecoin
A token pegged to the dollar, such as USDT or USDC.
