Vow.id for beginners

In five minutes you will know what it is about, what it costs and whether it suits you.

Shopas usual, e.g. on Amazon
Collect pointsthey are called Member Rewards (MR)
Use the discountlater, as a voucher

Not cashback: The points are discount rights. They are not money and not income.

The requirement

You need a wallet

Picture a locker on the internet. The wallet is the key to it. The wallet app on your phone is your key ring: just as your front door key and your car key hang on it, the key to your locker hangs in the app. At Vow.id you sign in with the key instead of a username and password.

Your IDYou sign in to Vow.id with the key.
Yours aloneOnly you have the key. No bank and no company has a copy.
12 wordsYour key, written down as 12 words. Anyone who knows them can open your locker.
Costs nothingThe key and the app are free. A small fee is charged only when you send or register.
Beyond Vow.id

Why a wallet of your own makes sense

Your bank account stays. The wallet is added to it: a place of your own for digital money that does not depend on a bank.

  • You manage it yourselfMoney in your account is managed by the bank for you. Digital money in the locker you manage yourself. A bank cannot freeze it. Exception: with some digital currencies, such as USDT or USDC, the issuer can freeze individual lockers.
  • Worldwide, around the clockYou can send to any country and receive from it at any time, including at night and at the weekend. Every transfer costs a small fee.
  • Not only eurosThe euro loses purchasing power over the years. Some digital currencies exist only in a fixed amount. That does not mean they keep their value: their price fluctuates sharply.
  • One key instead of many passwordsWith services like Vow.id you sign in with the key and do not need a new password.
  • Learning with a small stakeWith a few euros you can try out for yourself how digital money works.
The downside
  • You alone are responsible. Without the 12 words nobody can get into your locker any more, not even a support team.
  • The value of digital currencies fluctuates sharply. You can lose everything.
  • Start small and only put in money you do not need.
Honest status

What works today and what does not

Works
  • Signing in to Vow.id
  • Purchases at participating merchants are recorded
  • Recognised purchases appear as a proposed points amount
Does not work yet
  • The points first have to be reviewed. This review has not started yet.
  • Until then they are marked "Pending" and cannot be redeemed.
  • There is no date.

Vow.id is still in its test phase (beta). Not every purchase is recognised yet. You collect today and can redeem at the earliest once the review is running.

Your decision

Is this right for me?

Probably yes
  • You want to try out how digital money works anyway.
  • You shop online regularly.
  • You can put in an hour and a few euros without expecting anything back.
Probably not
  • You expect money in your account or an income.
  • You only want to use things that are finished and proven.
  • You do not want to keep 12 words safe yourself.

Waiting until redeeming works is also a sensible choice.

The one rule that protects everything

Never pass on your 12 words. No support team, no website and no acquaintance needs them. Not even the person who sent you this page.

If it suits you

Six steps to your first wallet

Have your smartphone, a pen and paper ready. Tick off what is done.

0 of 6

  1. Choose a wallet app

    2 min

    Our recommendation: TokenPocket. The app can handle BNB from the start. Trust Wallet or MetaMask work too.

    Can I switch apps later?

    Yes. Your Vow.id account is tied to your key for good, not to the app. In a new app you simply enter your 12 words. The key itself can no longer be changed at Vow.id.

  2. Install the app the right way

    5 min

    Go to TokenPocket's official website, tokenpocket.pro, and tap the link to the App Store or Play Store there.

    Do not search the store yourself and do not click on ads. There are fake apps that steal money.

  3. Create a new wallet for BNB

    5 min

    Open the app and choose to create a new wallet. The app asks for the network: choose BNB Smart Chain, also called "BSC" in the app. Then set a name and a password.

    The app now creates your key and shows it as 12 English words. These words are called the seed phrase.

    Depending on the app version you see the words straight away or only under "Back up" or "Backup". The exact button labels can differ.

  4. Write the 12 words on paper

    10 min

    By hand, numbered, in the right order. Many apps then ask you for the words as a check.

    Right
    Paper, two copies, two separate safe places.
    Never
    Screenshot, photo, notes app, email, cloud, WhatsApp. Never dictate or show them.

  5. Get a little BNB for the fee

    10–30 min

    BNB is a digital currency. You need it like postage: registering at Vow.id costs a small fee, and that fee is paid in BNB. There must be at least 0.002 BNB in the wallet. Take a little more. Because you chose BNB Smart Chain in step 3, your wallet is already set up for it.

    Important: When buying or receiving, always select "BNB Smart Chain". Sometimes it says "BSC" or "BEP20" there. That is the name of the network, much like a mobile phone network. On the wrong network the amount does not arrive and is often lost.
    Two ways to get BNB
    • Buy in the app: Tap "Buy" and pay by card or bank transfer. The purchase is handled by a payment service. You usually have to verify your identity once with an ID document, and for small amounts the fee is often high.
    • Have it sent to you: Tap "Receive" and choose BNB Smart Chain. The app shows your address: a long string of characters that starts with 0x. It is like an account number, and anyone may know it. Give it to someone who has BNB.

  6. Check that it has arrived

    a few min

    The amount appears in your app under "BNB". Only then do you carry on.

Wallet ready? Then carry on.

Your wallet is now prepared for BNB. The invitation link is in the quick start guide at step 1. Who invites you is fixed for good at registration. So open Vow.id through this link the very first time.

Good to know

What you risk

  • Up to this point: the small fee, your time and the data you provide.
  • You only put in more money if you later buy the paid version (Premium) or redeem points. To redeem you need the digital currency VOW. Both are voluntary.
  • Nobody guarantees that your points will be approved, that a merchant will accept the vouchers or that they will keep their value.
  • Digital currencies can lose a great deal of value, up to a total loss. What you have sent cannot be brought back.
  • Whoever invites you can receive a share of small discount rights from your purchases. Feel free to ask about it.
