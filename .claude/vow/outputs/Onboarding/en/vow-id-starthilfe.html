The essentials first

Vow.id is a web app that you log in to with your wallet. You provide proof of real purchases, for example at Amazon, and are proposed Member Rewards (MR) for them. Approved MR can be converted into v$ with a contribution in VOW; v$ are digital discount vouchers.

€0is what sign-up costs. You only pay a small BNB network fee.
0.002 BNBis the minimum that must be in the wallet (BNB Smart Chain).
Foreveris how long wallet, referrer and handle are fixed. None of them can be changed later.
Not cashbackMR are not money. To redeem them you add VOW worth half the market value of a v$.

What works today: sign-up, the receipt address, bank linking for Premium and the wallet scan. Purchases are recorded and shown as MR proposals.

What is not running yet: the review of proposals by independent verifiers. According to a notice from Vow.id, it is waiting for a smart contract that is to be deployed “shortly”. Until then MR stay on “Pending” and cannot be redeemed.

Vow.id is labelled as a beta. Not every receipt and not every bank is recognised yet.

Security first: Nobody needs your seed phrase, your private key, your email PIN or your bank code. Not even the person who invited you. Anyone who asks for them is trying to defraud you.

Sign-up in seven steps

Expect it to take about ten minutes. Keep only one Vow.id tab open while you do it. The app follows the language you have chosen, so the button labels depend on your setting; this guide uses the English labels.

  1. Open via the invitation link

    Open the link from your invitation in the browser, read the risk notice at the top and choose “Create Vow.id”. In the privacy settings you can decline advertising.

    Done when the wallet selection opens.

  2. Connect your wallet

    Choose your wallet or WalletConnect and confirm the connection in the wallet app. On a phone the browser may jump to the wallet app. Afterwards go back to the same browser tab.

    Done when your address appears with “Sign message to continue”.

  3. Check the referrer and sign the message

    Check the green line “Referred by …”. If the right name is shown there, sign the message. The signature costs nothing and moves no funds.

    Done when the “Join the Decentralised Affiliate Tree” screen appears.

  4. Join the affiliate tree

    Choose “Join and open wallet”, allow the switch to the BNB Smart Chain and confirm the transaction. This is the only fee. Wait for the confirmation and do not send a second transaction; press “Check again” instead.

    Done when your Vault opens with the email window.

  5. Confirm your email

    Enter your normal email address and type in the four-digit PIN from your inbox. Check your spam folder too.

    Done at “Email verified – Welcome to Vow.id”.

  6. Secure your handle

    Enter the name you want (3–30 lowercase letters, digits or hyphens), press “Check” and then “Claim”. The handle stays forever.

    Done when name@vow.id is shown in the overview.

  7. Prepare your first purchase

    Open Member Rewards → Checklist, choose your country and the providers you really use. Under “Receipts & MR rights”, switch on “Collect MR rights”. Then enter your name@vow.id as the account email at Amazon. Start with one provider.

    Done when a transaction appears under MR after your next real purchase.

Terms

BSC
Short for BNB Smart Chain, the network Vow.id runs on. Also called “BEP20” in some apps.
KYC
Identity check with an ID document, required by purchase services and exchanges.
DEX
Decentralised exchange such as PancakeSwap, where you swap tokens directly from your wallet.
2FA
Two-step sign-in: in addition to the wallet, a code from an authenticator app.
T&Cs
Vow.id's terms and conditions, called “Terms and conditions” on the site.
MR
Member Rewards. A free, non-transferable entry in a smart contract, permanently tied to your wallet.
Pending MR
Shown as “Pending” in the app. A proposal, not yet an approved reward. Has no monetary value and can be rejected.
Approved MR
Shown as “Active” in the app. Approved by three independent verifiers. Redeemable for 60 days, after which they expire.
Exercise
The official term for redeeming: converting approved MR into v$ against a voluntary VOW contribution.
v$
Voucher Dollar. A transferable right to propose or accept a $1 discount. Not money and not redeemable for money.
VOW
The token you contribute when redeeming MR.
BNB
The currency for network fees on the BNB Smart Chain.
Handle
Your name at Vow.id, and at the same time your receipt address name@vow.id.
Vault
Your data area in Vow.id for contact details, addresses and settings.
Affiliate tree
The referral directory on the blockchain. Every member has a fixed place in it.
Verifier
Independent reviewers who approve or challenge MR proposals (VV and QVV).
Plaid
The service provider through which Premium members connect their bank account in read-only mode.

What you should know before you put money in

Signing up and collecting MR cost nothing. You only put money in when you buy Premium or redeem MR with VOW.

  • Nobody guarantees that a proposal will be approved, that a merchant will accept v$ or that v$ will keep any particular value.
  • VOW that you contribute when redeeming is gone for good. It is not refunded.
  • VOW and v$ are crypto-assets. Their price can fall sharply, up to a total loss.
  • Transactions on the blockchain cannot be reversed.
  • Vow.id does not hold funds in custody and gives no investment advice. Neither does this page.
